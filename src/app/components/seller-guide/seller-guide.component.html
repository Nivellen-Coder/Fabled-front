Card Conditions

  • Excellent: Perfect condition, as if freshly pulled from a pack.
  • Near Mint: Very minimal wear, almost perfect. Cards in Near Mint condition may show subtle wear.
  • Good:

    A card in Good condition looks like it has gone through a long tournament without a sleeve.

    A card in good condition generally has significant wear on all areas, leaving white spots around the corners and edges. The surface of the card often has scratches and is slightly dirty. However, these are just normal signs of use. The card has no creases or water damage.

  • Lightly Played: Minor wear visible.
  • Played: Noticeable wear and minor imperfections. Cards in Played condition show obvious wear.
  • Heavily Played: Significant wear, may have creases or dents.
  • Poor: Heavy damage, not suitable for competitive play.

Understanding Magic Rarity Symbols

Magic: The Gathering cards have different rarity levels, indicated by their letter symbol.

  • Common - (C)
  • Uncommon - (U)
  • Rare - (R)
  • Mythic Rare - (M)
  • Promo/Special - Various colors depending on edition

Foil Types in Magic: The Gathering

Magic features various foil types that increase the collectability of cards:

  • Traditional Foil: Standard shiny effect across the card.
  • Etched Foil: Special matte finish with a unique shine.
  • Extended Art: Card border is removed for full-art display.
  • Borderless: Full artwork without borders.
