📦 Card Conditions

  • Excellent: Perfect condition, like new.
  • Near Mint: Slight signs of handling, nearly flawless.
  • Good:

    Noticeable play wear; white edges, corner wear, minor dirt or scratches.

    No bends, water damage, or major creases.

  • Lightly Played: Small imperfections, very minor edge wear.
  • Played: Visible wear, still tournament legal.
  • Heavily Played: Significant wear, may include bends or surface damage.
  • Poor: Torn, marked, or otherwise damaged — for collection only.

💎 Rarity Symbols Explained

Magic: The Gathering cards feature rarity indicators to denote their frequency:

  • Common – (C)
  • Uncommon – (U)
  • Rare – (R)
  • Mythic Rare – (M)
  • Promo/Special – Varies by set

✨ Foil Types

Different types of foils increase the collectability of your cards:

  • Traditional Foil: Classic shiny layer over artwork and card borders.
  • Etched Foil: Subtle textured shine, popular in collector editions.
  • Extended Art: Illustration extends beyond the border frame.
  • Borderless: Full-art with no black border — modern and sought after.
